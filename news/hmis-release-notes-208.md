Review recent updates and improvements to Open Path HMIS.

## Open Path 208

**Released:** April 16, 2026

### Updates

- **Length of stay field update.** We’ve updated how Length of Stay (LOS) is collected for Prior Living Situation B. LOS will now be collected in all cases, including when the prior living situation is homelessness, aligning with the latest Eva guidance. This change is not expected to impact reporting, but you may notice differences in Eva flags as a result.

---

## Open Path 207

**Released:** April 9, 2026

### Updates

- **Filters now persist across table navigation.** When you apply filters to tables such as Clients, Enrollments, or Projects, they remain in place as you move between pages, making it easier to return to or share a specific view.

---

## Open Path 203

**Released:** March 12, 2026

### Updates

- **Legacy referrals are now read-only.** You can no longer create or send new legacy referrals. Existing outgoing referrals remain available as history, and incoming referrals can still be reviewed and accepted or denied. Open Path HMIS will support direct referrals and waitlist-based referrals going forward.

---

## Open Path 201

**Released:** February 26, 2026

### Updates

- **Accessibility improvements.** We’ve implemented accessibility fixes across HMIS to improve WCAG 2.0 AA compliance and provide a more inclusive experience.

---

## Open Path 200

**Released:** February 19, 2026

### Updates

- **Streamlined workflow for project setup.** We’ve simplified the project setup form and clarified key fields so you can complete setup quickly without missing important information.

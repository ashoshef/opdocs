View a record of all changes made to an enrollment, including what was updated and who made the change.

## Access Audit History

1. Open the enrollment.
2. Click **Audit History** in the sidebar.

## Review Changes

1. Review the table to see all changes made to the enrollment.
2. Click **Fields Changed** for a row to view detailed updates.

## Filter Audit History

1. Click the **Filter** button.
2. Filter by Enrollment Record Type or User.
3. Apply the filters to update the table.

Remove a client record from HMIS when it was created in error or is no longer needed.

1. Search for the client record you want to delete.
2. Select the client from the search results.
3. Click **Update Client Details**.
4. Click **Delete Client** in the form navigation panel.
5. Click **Confirm** to permanently remove the client record.
6. Search for the client again to confirm the record no longer appears.

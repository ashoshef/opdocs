Review completed assessments, make updates, print a copy, or permanently delete an assessment from a client enrollment.

## Open an Assessment

1. Navigate to the assessments page for the client enrollment you want to review.
2. Select the assessment by clicking its row.
3. Review the read-only assessment. From this page, you can edit, print, or delete the assessment.

## Edit an Assessment

1. Click **Unlock Assessment**.
2. Make your changes, then click **Submit**.

## Print an Assessment

1. Click **Print** in the card on the left side of the page.
2. A print window and your browser's print dialog will open automatically. Print the assessment or save it as a PDF.
3. Close the print dialog using your browser controls, or click **Print View** to return to the assessment.

## Delete an Assessment

1. Click **Delete** in the card on the left side of the page.
2. In the confirmation window, click **Yes, Delete Assessment**.

Split one or more household members into a new enrollment while keeping the original enrollment intact.

1. Open the enrollment you want to update.
2. Click **Household** in the left sidebar.
3. Review the current household members.
4. Open the **More Actions** menu for the client you want to move into a new household.
5. Click **Split into New Household**.

> [[!NOTE]]
> Heads of household cannot be split into a new enrollment. The split option is disabled for the current head of household.

6. Select the household members to move into the new enrollment.
7. Click **Add Relationships**.
8. Define relationships between household members.

> [[!IMPORTANT]]
> You must assign a Head of Household for the new group before completing the split.

9. Click **Review Split**.
10. Review both households before confirming.
11. Click **Split Enrollment** to confirm, or go back to make changes.
12. Choose your next step from the confirmation options.
13. Verify the households are now separated in the enrollments view.

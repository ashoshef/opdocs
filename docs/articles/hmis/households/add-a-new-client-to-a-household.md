Create a new client and add them to an existing household within an enrollment.

1. From the enrollment overview page, click **Household** in the left panel.
2. Click **Add Household Member**.
3. Search for the client to confirm they do not already exist in HMIS.
4. If the client does not appear in the results, click **Add New Client**.
5. Select the client's Relationship to Head of Household.
6. Enter the client's profile information.
7. Click **Create and Enroll**.
8. Confirm the new client appears in the household list.
9. Continue by completing household intakes or updating the client's profile.

Add an existing client to a household within an enrollment.

1. Open the client's profile.
2. Click **Enrollments**.
3. Select the enrollment you want to update.
4. On the enrollment overview page, review the current household members.
5. Click **Add Household Member**.
6. Choose **Existing Client**.
7. Search for the client you want to add.
8. Click **Add to Household** next to the client.
9. Select the client's Relationship to Head of Household.
10. Click **Enroll**.
11. Confirm the client appears in the household list.

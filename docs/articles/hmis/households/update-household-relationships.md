Update a household member's relationship to the Head of Household within an existing enrollment.

1. Open the enrollment you want to update.
2. Click **Household** in the left sidebar.
3. Open the **More Actions** menu for the client you want to update.
4. Click **Change Relationship**.
5. Select the new relationship from the Relationship to Head of Household dropdown.
6. Click **Update**.
7. Confirm the updated relationship appears in the household list.

Update the head of household for an existing enrollment from the household page.

1. Open the enrollment you want to update.
2. Click **Household** in the left sidebar.
3. Click the **More Actions** menu for the household member you want to set as head of household.
4. Click **Make Head of Household**.
5. Review the confirmation message.

> While the system allows a minor to be selected as Head of Household, this should only be used for unaccompanied or parenting youth. If an adult is present in the household, select the adult as Head of Household to ensure accurate reporting and eligibility.

6. Click **Confirm**.
7. Confirm the updated head of household appears in the household list.

Delete an enrollment if it was created in error or should not be included in reporting.

> [[!WARNING]]
> Only delete an enrollment if it was created in error. Deleting an enrollment permanently removes associated data, cannot be undone, and may impact reporting. If the enrollment is valid, update the record instead of deleting it.

1. Go to the Enrollments page for the project where the enrollment is located.
2. Select the enrollment you want to delete.
3. Determine whether the enrollment has a submitted Intake Assessment, then follow the appropriate workflow.

## Delete an Enrollment Without a Submitted Intake Assessment

1. On the enrollment overview page, click **Delete Enrollment**.
2. Click **Yes, Delete Enrollment** to confirm.

## Delete an Enrollment With a Submitted Intake Assessment

1. In the enrollment, click **Assessments**.
2. Open the submitted **Intake Assessment**.
3. Click **Delete**.
4. Click **Yes, Delete Enrollment** to confirm.
5. Confirm the enrollment no longer appears in the list.

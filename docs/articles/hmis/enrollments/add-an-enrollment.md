Create an enrollment for either an existing client or a new client from the Enrollments page.

1. Go to **Projects** and select the project where you want to create the enrollment.
2. You'll be navigated automatically to the Enrollments page. Click **Add Enrollment**.
3. Search for the client to confirm whether they already exist in HMIS.
4. Choose the workflow that matches the client.

## Enroll an Existing Client

1. Select the client from the search results.
2. Review the required fields. **Entry Date** defaults to today, and **Relationship to Head of Household** defaults to self or head of household.
3. Click **Enroll**.

## Enroll a New Client

1. If no matching client is found, click **Add New Client**.
2. Complete the **Enroll a New Client** form.
3. Click **Create and Enroll Client**.
4. Add household members if needed, or click **Back to Project** to finish.
5. Confirm the enrollment appears in the enrollments table.

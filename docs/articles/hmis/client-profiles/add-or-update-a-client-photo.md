Add or update a client photo from the client profile page in HMIS.

## Add a Client Photo

1. Search for the client you want to update.
2. Select the client row to open the client profile.
3. Check the client profile to see whether a photo is already on file.
4. Hover over the photo area, then click to add or update the client photo.
5. Drag and drop an image file, or click to upload one.
6. If you choose to upload a file, select the image you want to use, then click **Open**.
7. Review the image preview, then click **Save**. To remove the selected file before saving, click **Clear**.
8. Confirm the updated photo appears in the left navigation and on the client profile page.

## Change a Client's Profile Photo

1. Open the client's profile.
2. Click the current photo or photo area to update it.
3. Upload a new image. The modal displays the current photo and a preview of the new image.
4. Click **Save** to apply the new photo. To choose a different image, click **Clear** under the file name.
5. Confirm the updated photo appears on the client profile.

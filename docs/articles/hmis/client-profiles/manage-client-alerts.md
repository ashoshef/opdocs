Create alerts on a client's profile to share important information with anyone who can view that record.

1. Review the available alert priority levels. Alerts display on the client profile in priority order. Check with your organization for how each level should be used.
2. Open the client's profile.
3. Click **Create Alert**.
4. Complete all required fields in the alert form.
5. Select a priority level from the dropdown.
6. Enter the alert note you want to display.
7. Add an expiration date. The alert is removed automatically when the date is reached.
8. Click **Create Alert**.
9. Confirm the alert appears on the client profile.
10. To delete an alert, click the trash icon on the alert.
11. Click **Yes, delete Alert** in the confirmation window.
12. Confirm the alert is removed from the client profile.

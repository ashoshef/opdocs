Services track the assistance provided to a client during an enrollment, such as case management, bed nights, or financial support. Access and manage services from the Services section within the enrollment. Some services are required based on funder and project type settings, and custom services may also be available depending on your community configuration.

## Manage Services in an Enrollment

1. In the enrollment, click **Services** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Add Service**.
3. Select the service type you want to add.
4. Choose the appropriate service for the client's enrollment.
5. Enter the Date Provided (required) and complete any additional fields.
6. Click **Add Service** to save.
7. Select a service from the list to update or delete it.
8. To update a service, make changes and click **Save Changes**.
9. To delete a service, click **Delete** in the service window.
10. Click **Yes, Delete Service** to confirm. This action cannot be undone.

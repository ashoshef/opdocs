Organizations group related projects together. Projects are where enrollments, assessments, and services are managed, so most communities create the organization first and then add projects underneath it.

> [[!NOTE]]
> Creating an organization requires admin access, even though organizations are accessed from the **Projects** page.

## Create an Organization

1. Go to **Projects**, then click **View Organizations**.
2. Click **+ Add Organization**.
3. Enter the required details, then click **Create Organization**. You'll be taken to the organization's detail page.

Once an organization exists, see [Create and Manage Projects](create-and-manage-projects.md) to add projects to it.
